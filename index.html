    Hide & Seek

    One player seeks. Everyone else draws a hider, tucks it into the scene and keeps moving from room to room. Each room change earns the hider a point, but moving takes time and the seeker can spot you on the way. Up to 10 players, and everyone gets a turn as the seeker.
